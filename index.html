Our game

Nostos

Third-person exploration · Windows and Linux · In development

You play Penelope, stranded on a large island with no memory of how she got there. Explore forests and lakes under a day and night sky, and find your way home.

  • The island is generated from a fixed seed, so every player gets the same world
  • Low-poly terrain with a different ground palette for each biome
  • Trees, rocks, mushrooms and wind-blown grass that bends away from you
  • Animated sea and lakes, with aurora, stars, sun and moon
